HOW TO MEASURE YOUR DRIP

1. CHEST Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the measuring tape horizontal and relaxed.
2. LENGTH Measure from the highest point of the shoulder collar down to the bottom hemline.
3. WAIST Measure around your natural waistline where your pants typically sit comfortably.
